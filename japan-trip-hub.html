Itinerary Notes
Why timing matters: several of Japan's most popular experiences use timed-release or lottery ticketing 1–3 months out, and November 2026 is the 25th‑anniversary year for Universal Studios Japan — expect faster sellouts than usual. Check items off as your group books them; this list saves in the page.
ENTER LOTTERY IN AUGUST 2026 — RESULTS SEPT 1

Nintendo Museum, Uji (Kyoto)

Tickets are lottery-only via museum-tickets.nintendo.com (free Nintendo Account needed). Entries open 3 months before the target month, with results on the 1st of the following month — so for a November visit, enter during August 2026, results announced Sept 1. No walk-ins, no third-party booking. If you miss the lottery, unclaimed slots sometimes open for direct purchase about 2 weeks after results — worth checking the site then too.

BOOK BY ~ MID-SEPTEMBER 2026

Universal Studios Japan (Osaka) — Express Pass

Express Passes for November typically go on sale mid-September (about 6–7 weeks out) and sell out weeks in advance online; on-site sales are rare. Base Studio Pass (park admission) is separate and required alongside it. 2026 is USJ's 25th anniversary ("Discover U!!"), so crowds and demand are higher than normal — book as soon as the window opens via the official USJ site or Klook/Rakuten.

BOOK: AS SOON AS CALENDAR OPENS (~EARLY SEPT), AT LEAST 2–3 WEEKS AHEAD

teamLab Planets / Borderless (Tokyo)

Timed-entry tickets, booking calendar opens roughly two months ahead. Planets does not sell on-site at all; Borderless same-day tickets can also sell out. Rough pricing: Planets ¥2,600–4,200 adult depending on date; Borderless ¥3,800–5,600 adult. Weekends need 2–3 weeks' notice, holiday-adjacent dates a month+. Book via the official site or Klook (often best rates).

BOOK ON: OCT 10, 2026, 10:00 AM JST

Ghibli Museum, Mitaka (Tokyo)

Overseas tickets are released via Lawson Ticket International on the 10th of the month for the following month — so November tickets go on sale Oct 10 at 10am Japan time. They sell out within minutes/hours. Someone in the group should be ready right at 10am JST that day. Fallback: JTB Sunrise Tour packages if you miss the window.

OPENS ~2 WEEKS AHEAD OF EACH DATE, AT MIDNIGHT JST

Shibuya Sky — sunset slot

Advance tickets ¥2,500 (vs ¥3,000 on the door). Dates open roughly 2 weeks out; the slot starting about an hour before sunset is the most popular and can go within days. Book the moment your date opens. Heads-up: the official site has been rejecting a lot of foreign-issued cards since mid-2024 — Klook is a safer bet for booking with a non-Japanese card.

GET THE IDP BEFORE YOU LEAVE HOME — CANNOT BE DONE IN JAPAN

Mario Kart street tour

Every participant needs an International Driving Permit (1949 Geneva Convention, booklet form — not a printout) plus their physical home driver's licence and passport. The IDP has to be issued in your home country before you fly out; it cannot be obtained once you're in Japan. (UK: apply at a Post Office. Handful of countries — Switzerland, Germany, France, Taiwan, Belgium, Estonia, Monaco — can use an official Japanese translation of their licence instead.) Book the tour itself separately once everyone's permits are sorted.

BOOK RESERVED SEATS ~3–4 WEEKS AHEAD

Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka transport

Skip the JR Pass for this route — with just Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka, a round-trip Tokyo–Kyoto Shinkansen fare (~¥28,340) is well under the 7-day Pass price (¥50,000 now, rising to ¥53,000 from Oct 1, 2026). The Pass only pays off if you add a longer leg like Hiroshima or Kanazawa. Book point-to-point reserved seats instead via SmartEX or JR-Central/JR-West's Ekinet, ideally 3–4 weeks out — November is peak autumn travel season and reserved cars do sell out on popular days/times.

HEADS-UP: LIKELY WON'T ALIGN WITH THESE DATES

Baseball at Tokyo Dome

Worth flagging early: the 2026 NPB regular season and Japan Series are on track to wrap up by around November 1 (Japan Series currently slated Oct 24 – Nov 1 if it goes the full 7 games). That's before your Nov 4 arrival, so an actual NPB game during the trip looks unlikely. Worth a quick check closer to the time for any exhibition games or other Tokyo Dome events, but don't bank on this one — may be worth dropping from the plan.

BOOK 1–8 WEEKS AHEAD DEPENDING ON RESTAURANT

Sought-after restaurant reservations

Popular sushi/omakase counters (like the small 6-seat spots in Osaka) often book out weeks to months ahead. Use TableCheck, Pocket Concierge, or the OMAKASE app for English-friendly booking; some places only take reservations via phone or a hotel concierge. Worth locking in a few "must-do" dinners early — Gonpachi included — and leaving other nights flexible for Dotonbori/Omoide Yokocho-style wandering.

CHECK/BOOK IN OCTOBER

Kyoto autumn night illuminations

Temples like Kiyomizu-dera, Eikando, and Kodai-ji run special autumn night openings with timed tickets, usually released about a month ahead — check each temple's site directly in October since dates/rules change yearly.

Foliage timing heads-up: current 2026 forecasts point to Kyoto's peak autumn colour landing around Nov 20–Dec 7 — slightly later than average. Your Nov 4–18 window likely catches early-to-mid colour rather than full peak; Arashiyama and Tōfuku-ji tend to turn earliest, so they're your best bet, especially for the traveler leaving Nov 11.

Food

Dotonbori — takoyaki, nightlife and neon; Osaka's signature food strip, best after dark for the neon-on-canal look.
Yakiniku dinner — plan one night for this.
Kuromon Ichiba Market — covered market, 150+ stalls; sea urchin, grilled oysters, wagyu skewers, tuna sashimi. Open ~9am–6pm, closed some Sundays.
Okonomiyaki at Mizuno or Chibo — two of the longest-standing, most-recommended spots for the dish.
Shinsekai — retro district where kushikatsu originated; cheaper and less touristy than Dotonbori.
Small-counter sushi (e.g. Jinsei, Sakae Sushi) — Jinsei is a 6-seat counter praised for watching the chef work; Sakae Sushi rated as excellent value.
